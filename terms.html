What the tool is

Respawn Gear Co Automation is a personal tool used to send video content to the Respawn Gear Co TikTok account (@respawngearco). It is not a public app or service. Nobody else signs up for it or uses it.

What connecting it allows

By connecting this tool to a TikTok account, you agree that it may upload video content to that account's drafts, using TikTok's own Login Kit and Content Posting API. Every video is reviewed by a person before it's sent, and posting from the drafts is done by hand in the TikTok app.

Removing access

You can remove the tool's access at any time from your TikTok account settings. After that it can't send anything.

Videos and links

Repair and mod videos show what worked for us. Follow them at your own risk, and take care with tools, heat and electronics. Some posts link to products on Amazon; as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.
