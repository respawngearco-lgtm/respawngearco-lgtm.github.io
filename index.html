How it started

It started with a PS5 controller with stick drift. A new one cost too much, so we opened it up and fixed it. That led to softmodding a PS4, an Xbox 360 and a PS Vita, and a bench that never seems to be empty. Respawn is where we share what worked, what didn't, and what it cost.

What we post

Controller repair

Stick drift, sticky buttons, dead triggers. Most of it is a cheap fix if you know where to look.

Console cleaning and care

Loud fans, overheating, old thermal paste. Simple jobs that add years to a console.

Softmods and homebrew

What we've modded, how it went, and what to watch out for before you try it.

Gear that holds up

Parts and tools we actually use on the bench, like hall effect stick kits that don't drift.

How we post to TikTok

We built a small private tool, Respawn Gear Co Automation, to get videos from the workbench onto our own TikTok account. It only works with our account (@respawngearco). There's no sign-up and nobody else uses it.

  1. We film on the bench. Clips go into our own storage at home.
  2. A draft gets made. The tool writes a caption, adds captions to the video, and suggests the parts or tools shown. AI helps with the first draft.
  3. A person checks every post. We watch each video, fix the caption, and confirm every product before anything moves on. Nothing goes out without that check.
  4. It's sent to our TikTok drafts. We connect with TikTok's Login Kit (basic profile info) and send the video with TikTok's Content Posting API (upload to drafts). It lands in our TikTok inbox as a draft.
  5. We post it ourselves from the TikTok app, with TikTok's AI-generated content label turned on whenever a video uses an AI voice.

You can read how we handle data in our Privacy Policy and the rules for the tool in our Terms of Service.
